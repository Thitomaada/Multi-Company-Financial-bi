Key findings

  • Growth is strong, profit is thin. Group revenue is up about 12% year to date (Jan–Sep 2026 vs 2025), but EBITDA margin is only 4.7% overall.
  • Solstice (SaaS) is the growth and turnaround story: revenue +21.5%, EBITDA margin moved from −2.2% to +9.4%.
  • Kessler (food) is the risk: 19.9% gross margin, 1.9% EBITDA margin, and cash turned negative by Sep 2026.
  • Margin squeeze at Helix: unit costs rose faster than prices, so gross margin slipped from 31.4% to 29.9%.
  • Budgets were too optimistic: every company missed budget revenue by 2–4% and EBITDA by 28–58%.
  • Costs matter more than price in the scenarios: a 5% COGS rise costs more EBITDA than a 1% price rise adds, especially at Kessler.
